### English-language transcription

**PDF page 3, item 2 — Amendment of First Schedule:**

> The Biosafety Act 2007, which is referred to as the “principal Act” in this Order, is amended in the English language text, in subparagraph 13(2) of the First Schedule, by substituting for the words “Lembaga Biokeselamatan Negara” the words “Lembaga Biokeselamatan Kebangsaan”.

**PDF page 3, item 3 — Amendment of Third Schedule:**

> The Third Schedule to the principal Act is amended by inserting after paragraph 8 the following paragraph:

> “9. Any enforcement officer as defined in section 3 of the Malaysian Quarantine and Inspection Services Act 2011 [Act 728].”.

## Reconciliation result

The instrument makes two operative changes: (1) it substitutes “Lembaga Biokeselamatan Kebangsaan” for “Lembaga Biokeselamatan Negara” in the English-language text of First Schedule subparagraph 13(2); and (2) it inserts Third Schedule paragraph 9, defining the relevant enforcement officer by reference to section 3 of the Malaysian Quarantine and Inspection Services Act 2011 [Act 728].

The target of this instrument is the First and Third Schedules to the Biosafety Act 2007 [Act 678], not the First Schedule or Regulation 16 of the Biosafety (Approval and Notification) Regulations 2010 [P.U. (A) 367/2010]. Comparison against the staged 2010 Regulations and the claim locators therefore shows that this instrument does not, on its face, amend the First Schedule technique/exemption criteria relied on by CLM-007. It also does not, on its face, amend Regulation 16 or create a notification requirement relied on by CLM-005. This is an impact observation only, not a conclusion that either 2010 provision is current, applicable, or sufficient to support a determination. The amendment does not by itself establish the complete current amendment history of the Act or Regulations.

The staged Act 678 extraction identifies the schedule section beginning at PDF page 113, and the existing Third Schedule officer list ends with paragraph 8 on PDF page 118. The First Schedule subparagraph 13(2) before/after wording was reconciled by visual comparison because the native Act extraction does not provide a sufficiently reliable exact text span for that subparagraph. The BioSafe project owner accepted the transcription as transcribed on 13 September 2026. These schedule-boundary observations are recorded in the structured reconciliation artifact; they do not promote any claim.
