BIOLOGICAL SAFETY SOP — TEST FIXTURE

Purpose:
This SOP describes routine handling of a non-recombinant bacterial culture in a research laboratory.

Procedure summary:
Personnel wear laboratory coats and gloves and perform routine culture handling on the bench.

Known information:
- Organism identity is provided in the study record.
- The activity is research-only.
- No genetic modification is described in this fixture.

Information intentionally omitted:
- exposure route assessment
- scale/quantity
- facility-specific containment details
- decontamination validation
- waste-disposal details
