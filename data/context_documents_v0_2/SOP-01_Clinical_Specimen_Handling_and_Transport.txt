1. Purpose
This SOP describes the receipt, temporary storage, handling, packaging and internal transfer of clinical specimens received by the Research Laboratory for research purposes.
2. Scope
This procedure applies to staff and students who handle human clinical specimens that may contain infectious agents. It covers receipt, processing and transfer to another laboratory.
3. Responsibilities
All personnel are responsible for handling specimens carefully and wearing appropriate PPE. The laboratory manager oversees the procedure.
4. Specimen Receipt
Specimens should be checked when received. If a package appears damaged, staff should handle it carefully and inform the laboratory manager.
5. Risk Assessment
Specimens are considered potentially infectious and should be handled at an appropriate biosafety level. The laboratory will follow relevant biosafety requirements.
6. PPE and Work Practices
Laboratory coats, gloves and eye protection should be used as appropriate. Personnel should follow good microbiological practice and avoid unnecessary exposure.
7. Packaging and Internal Transfer
Specimens transferred to another laboratory must be placed in suitable leak-proof packaging and labelled appropriately. The receiving laboratory should be informed before transfer.
8. Spill or Exposure Incident
Any spill or exposure should be reported immediately. The area should be cleaned and disinfected using an appropriate method.
9. Waste
Used materials should be disposed of as clinical waste according to laboratory practice.
10. Training and Records
Personnel should receive appropriate training. Records should be maintained by the laboratory manager.
11. References
WHO Laboratory Biosafety Manual. Malaysian guidance should be followed where applicable.
