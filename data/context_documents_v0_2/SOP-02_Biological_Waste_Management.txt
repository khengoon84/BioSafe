1. Purpose
This SOP provides general instructions for management of biological and clinical waste generated during laboratory research.
2. Scope
The SOP applies to biological waste generated in the laboratory, including contaminated consumables and specimens.
3. Responsibilities
All laboratory users must dispose of waste correctly. The laboratory manager is responsible for arranging collection.
4. Segregation
Waste should be separated into appropriate containers. Sharps should be placed in sharps containers. Other biological waste should be placed in clinical-waste bags.
5. Decontamination
Waste may be decontaminated before disposal when necessary. Appropriate methods should be used.
6. Temporary Storage
Filled bags should be tied securely and stored in the waste area until collection. Waste should not be kept for too long.
7. Collection and Disposal
Waste will be collected by an approved contractor and disposed of appropriately. The laboratory manager will keep the contractor details.
8. Spills
Spills should be cleaned promptly using disinfectant. Gloves must be worn.
9. Training
New staff will be shown the waste procedure during induction.
10. Records
Collection records should be maintained.
11. References
Environmental Quality Act 1974; Environmental Quality (Scheduled Wastes) Regulations 2005; laboratory waste guidance.
