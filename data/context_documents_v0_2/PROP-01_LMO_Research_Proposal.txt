1. Project Title
Evaluation of a genetically modified microorganism for recombinant protein expression.
2. Background
The project will investigate expression of a recombinant protein using a genetically modified microorganism. The work is intended to support development of a laboratory research platform.
3. Objectives
1. Generate the recombinant microorganism. 2. Evaluate recombinant protein expression. 3. Compare expression under selected laboratory conditions.
