1. Purpose
This SOP describes routine laboratory work involving a genetically modified microorganism (GMM) used for research.
2. Scope
It applies to researchers working with the GMM in the laboratory and covers culture, experimental manipulation, storage and waste.
3. Project Information
The GMM contains a recombinant construct used to study protein expression. The exact inserted sequence and host characteristics are described in the research proposal.
4. Biosafety Oversight
The Principal Investigator is responsible for ensuring that the project complies with applicable biosafety requirements. The work will be performed at the appropriate biosafety level.
5. Risk Assessment
The project has been assessed as low risk because the work is contained and the microorganism is used only in small quantities.
6. Access and Security
Only laboratory personnel should enter the laboratory. Samples are stored in the laboratory refrigerator.
7. Laboratory Practices
Personnel must follow standard microbiological practices and use appropriate PPE. Procedures should be performed carefully.
8. Waste
GMM cultures and contaminated materials should be inactivated before disposal. Waste should then be disposed of according to institutional procedures.
9. Transport and Storage
The GMM may be moved between rooms in sealed containers. Storage containers should be labelled.
10. Incidents
Any accident, spill or loss of material should be reported to the Principal Investigator.
11. Documentation
Relevant approvals and records should be maintained by the laboratory.
12. References
Biosafety Act 2007 (Act 678); Biosafety (Approval and Notification) Regulations 2010; Malaysian contained-use biosafety guidance; WHO Laboratory Biosafety Manual.
