BIO-SAFE SYNTHETIC FORM E FIELD MAP — BENCHMARK ONLY
This is not an official Malaysian government form. It is a structured benchmark representation
used only to test whether BioSafe transfers proposal information conservatively.

Researcher-facing fields evaluated:
A1 Project / activity title
A2 Applicant / principal investigator and institution
A3 Objectives / purpose of the contained-use activity
A4 LMO / GMM identity and characteristics, including host and inserted construct where available
A5 Proposed activities, facility / premises, containment and risk-management information
A6 Duration / dates, waste, transport / storage, emergency response and relevant references

Benchmark drafting rule:
- Transfer only information explicitly supported by the proposal.
- If a relevant field is absent, insert exactly:
  [Information not provided — confirmation required]
- Do not invent host species, inserted sequence, containment level, facility certification,
  risk classification, regulatory determination, approval, notification status or IBC decision.
- BioSafe may identify possible regulatory pathways, but must not simulate an official
  institutional or regulatory determination.
